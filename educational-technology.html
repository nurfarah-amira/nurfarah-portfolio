PROJECT DETAILS

Educational Technology & Digital Learning

A collection of digital learning and educational technology projects developed using interactive content, multimedia, AI tools, and virtual production technologies.

Project Overview

This project involved developing and producing various digital learning resources during my internship at the Centre of Open and Distance Learning (CODL), UTeM.

The work covered educational technology, interactive learning content, multimedia production, and virtual production training materials.

Projects & Technologies

Articulate Rise H5P Colossyan AI Rokoko Studio SimLab Virtual Production

My Contribution

  • Created user manuals and learning materials for Articulate Rise.
  • Developed interactive H5P learning content for digital learning.
  • Produced and edited educational multimedia resources and tutorial videos.
  • Worked with Rokoko Studio, SimLab, and Virtual Production technologies.
  • Explored AI-based tools such as Colossyan AI for educational content creation.

Key Learning

This experience strengthened my skills in digital content development, multimedia production, interactive learning design, and emerging educational technologies.
